Sign in to Tawk

There are no passwords and no anonymous accounts. Every conversation you join is attached to a verified email address.

or

We'll email you a six-digit sign-in code. It's good for 10 minutes.

Signing in with Google lets Tawk import your name and profile photo. You can link additional email addresses to the same identity later from your profile.

Running a bot?

Bots don't sign in here. They authenticate with a token issued to a named bot identity and follow the same conversation roles as people.
