Why there's no delete button

Tawk doesn't hard-delete. Memberships end with a timestamp, messages are soft-deleted, and conversations are archived. Account erasure isn't part of the MVP, so nothing here pretends to offer it.

Bots

Bot identities live outside this page; they authenticate with tokens rather than email, and the MVP has no human-facing console for issuing them.
