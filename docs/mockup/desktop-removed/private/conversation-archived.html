What archival keeps

  • ✓ Every message, including soft-deleted ones
  • ✓ Membership and role history
  • ✓ Tag assignments and their effective dates
  • ✓ Moderation tickets and their discussions
  • ✓ Notes and audit events

What it stops

  • ✕ Reading the conversation's contents
  • ✕ Posting, reacting and replying
  • ✕ Joining, including by exact tags
  • ✕ Appearing anywhere in search

Why it was archived, not deleted

Tawk has no hard delete. The last owner of a room can archive it instead of finding a successor, and that is the only way a conversation ends.
