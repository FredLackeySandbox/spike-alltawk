The concept behind that Tawk application is that any person or bot can create or join a conversation using a combination of tags. When a conversation is created it is given at least one tag which may or may not be easily guessed. A tag can be a simple format such as "{name}" or a complex format of "{key}:{value}". Each conversation can have up to 10 tags. The person or bot that created the conversation is considered the owner of the conversation and can add and remove tags at will. They can also promote other members to an owner or admin role in the conversation. They are also able to determine if the conversation is "listed". If a conversation is not listed it will never show up in search results and the user will only know if the conversation they attempted to join matches their list of tags when they click the "join" button. If it is listed, people may search for conversations by clicking on tag pills to add or remove them from their search criteria. This will cause the list of matching conversations to show and they may join any of the conversations they found.
