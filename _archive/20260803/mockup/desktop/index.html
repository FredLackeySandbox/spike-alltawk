Not built, on purpose

Bot Access is the sixth application area, but AREA_LAYOUTS.md declares it a machine-to-machine surface with no persistent navigation and warns against inferring a developer console or token-management UI. It has no screens here. Bots appear as ordinary participants throughout.

No pricing, no plans

REQUIREMENTS.md lists payments as None and puts paid plans and tiers out of scope. There is no pricing page, no subscription screen and no upgrade prompt anywhere in this mockup.

How to read it

You're signed in as Dana Okafor: owner of one room, administrator of another, an ordinary member elsewhere, and suspended in one. That mix is deliberate: it makes every permission boundary visible in one pass.
