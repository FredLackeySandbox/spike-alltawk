Suggestions only ever include tags that at least one listed conversation uses. A tag that lives only on unlisted rooms never appears here.

How matching works

  • Listed Appears when your search touches any of its tags.
  • Exclusive Appears only when you've selected all of its tags.
  • Unlisted Never named. Joins only on an exact tag-set match, with nothing extra.

Archived conversations never appear in search, and a conversation you've been banned from can't be rejoined.
